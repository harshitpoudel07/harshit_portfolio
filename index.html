Hi, I'm
Harshit Poudel.

I’m Harshit Poudel, from Narayanghat, Chitwan, Nepal. I enjoy riding, traveling to new places, playing games, and exploring anything that catches my curiosity. I like discovering new roads, experiencing different cultures and places, learning about unfamiliar topics, and trying things that take me outside my usual routine. Whether I’m out on a ride, planning my next trip, spending time gaming, or researching something interesting, I’m always looking for a new experience and a good memory to take with me. I believe life becomes more meaningful when we stay curious, meet different people, explore new places, and keep learning from every experience. This website is my little corner of the internet where I can share my interests, photos, adventures, and the moments that make me who I am.
