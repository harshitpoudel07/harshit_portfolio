Hi, I'm Harshit.

I’m Harshit Poudel, from Narayanghat, Chitwan, Nepal. I enjoy riding, traveling to new places, playing games, and exploring anything that catches my curiosity. I like discovering new roads, experiencing different cultures and places, learning about unfamiliar topics, and trying things that take me outside my usual routine. Whether I’m out on a ride, planning my next trip, spending time gaming, or researching something interesting, I’m always looking for a new experience and a good memory to take with me. This website is a little space to share my interests, moments, and the things that make me who I am.

About Me

Curious by nature and always ready for the next adventure. I enjoy the freedom of riding, the excitement of traveling somewhere new, and the fun of gaming when it’s time to relax. I’m also someone who likes asking questions, researching random topics, learning new things, and seeing life from different perspectives. I believe every place has a story, every experience teaches us something, and even the smallest moments can become great memories. I’m still learning, growing, and discovering what I enjoy most, one ride, one trip, and one new experience at a time.
